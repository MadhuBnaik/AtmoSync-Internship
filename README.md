# AtmoSync
### Micro-Climate Arbitrage Analytics – Data Analytics Internship Project

## 📌 Project Overview
AtmoSync is a data analytics project focused on understanding avocado market prices and cold-chain transportation conditions. The project explores how factors such as temperature, humidity, delivery delays, and transportation efficiency are related to product freshness and spoilage risk.

The goal is to analyze the data, identify useful patterns, and generate insights that can help improve cold-chain delivery operations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze avocado prices across different regions and types.
- Compare conventional and organic avocado prices.
- Study temperature and humidity conditions during transportation.
- Understand the relationship between temperature and spoilage risk.
- Compare on-time, at-risk, and delayed deliveries.
- Analyze delivery freshness and energy consumption.
- Present findings through visualizations and dashboards.

## 📊 Key Analysis and Findings

### Avocado Market Analysis
- Compared conventional and organic avocado prices.
- Explored price trends across different years.
- Analyzed price differences across regions.

### Cold Chain Analysis
- Studied the relationship between transportation temperature and spoilage risk.
- Observed a positive correlation of approximately **0.639** between temperature and spoilage risk.
- Compared spoilage risk across different delivery statuses.

### Delivery Efficiency Analysis
- Compared estimated freshness across delivery statuses.
- Analyzed energy consumption for on-time, at-risk, and delayed deliveries.
- Observed that on-time deliveries had higher average freshness and lower average energy consumption than delayed deliveries.
